Listener「手伝って『あげた』……？」 That could sound a little condescending.

Why“〜te ageru” puts “I did this for you” up front. In an interview, it can sound a little condescending.

Say it like this「店長が忙しいとき、
手伝いました。」

FAQ

How is this different from AI apps?

A Japanese person actually listens to your voice and shows you, with your own words, where they lost track. No polite compliments.

Who listens?

Native speakers of Japanese — not teachers, but ordinary Japanese listeners. Each result is the record of the person who listened that time; others may hear it differently.

When can I take it?

Right now. Before launch, the first 20 people take it free. Results go out in order (it may take a few days).

How much will it cost?

It’s free for now. After paid launch, the plan is ¥2,980 per test, paid after you finish speaking.

Do I need to prepare?

No. Just answer the spoken questions on the spot. A quiet place and a phone are enough.

Is the level a certificate?

No. It’s a guide based on CEFR stages. Instead, your answers show what’s missing for the next level.

Will my accent cost me points?

Not if you were understood. What matters is whether you got your meaning across.

What happens to my recording?

It's stored on computers we manage in Japan and used to make your result. To have it deleted, email hello@aunjapanese.com and we'll delete the original data. See our privacy policy.
