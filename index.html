FAQ

How much does it cost?

It's free for now.

Does a Japanese person listen every time?

Only when you ask, after the test. You can ask once a week, for free. Results arrive in a few days in the app or by email. Right after you submit, you get an AI review (no scores).

Who listens?

Namba, AUN's founder. Not a Japanese teacher: an everyday Japanese listener.

What happens to my recordings?

They're stored on Google Cloud in Tokyo. Sessions you asked us to listen to are deleted 30 days after your result; the rest after 1 year. You can ask us to delete them anytime.
