  4. お問い合わせ：メールアドレス、内容、表示言語、日時。

  1. 結果（伝わらなかった所、その理由、言い換えの例、レベルの目安）をつくり、お届けするため

  5. 利用状況の分析のため（個人を特定しない統計として）

5-2. Cookie と外部への送信

このサイトでは、利用状況を分析するため、Google LLC の Google アナリティクスを使っています。ページを開くと、次の情報が Google に送られます：見たページの URL、どこから来たか、日時、ブラウザ・端末の種類、画面の大きさ、IP アドレス、Cookie による閲覧の識別子。名前やメールアドレスは送りません。Google はこの情報を、同社のプライバシーポリシーに従って扱います。

欧州経済領域・英国・スイスからのアクセスでは、同意をいただくまで分析用の Cookie を使いません。計測を止めたいときは、Google アナリティクス オプトアウト アドオンを使うか、ブラウザで Cookie を無効にしてください。ブラウザには、このほか表示言語の設定と、同意の選択を保存します。

6. 保存期間

期間を過ぎた情報や、目的を果たして不要になった情報は、委託先とバックアップを含めて削除します。削除のご請求があったときは、元のデータを削除し、以後の学習には使いません。ただし、すでに学習を終えた AI から特定の方の影響だけを取り除くことは、技術的に難しい場合があります。

7. 安全管理のための措置

  • 個人情報の取り扱いの責任者を置き、取り扱いのルールを定め、担当者に教えます。
  • 情報にアクセスできる人を、必要な担当者に限ります。
  • 録音などを扱う端末は、パスワードと暗号化で守り、持ち出しを管理します。
  • 漏えいなどが起きたときは、すぐに調べ、法令に従って個人情報保護委員会とご本人に報告します。
  • 聞き手には、担当する録音だけを渡し、秘密を守る約束をしてもらいます。
  • データベースとの通信を暗号化し、ブラウザから他人の情報を読めない設定にしています。
  • 上の5に書いた外国の制度と、各事業者の措置を確かめたうえで利用します。

1900 Co., Ltd. (株式会社1900, “we”) handles personal information in AUN (阿吽, “the Service”) in accordance with Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other laws, as follows.

1. Who we are

2. What we collect

  1. Early sign-up: email, first language, country, JLPT level, free text, display language, browser information (user agent), time of sign-up, and which link you came from.
  2. Taking the test (including free trials): email, your voice recordings, their transcripts, timing records, consent records, device and browser information.
  3. Making your result: the listener’s notes and comments.
  4. Contact: email, message, display language, time.
  5. Site usage: display language, which link you came from, how far you scrolled, whether you pressed a button, time, screen width. We store this separately from, and do not link it to, your name or email. A number that changes every time you open the page groups actions within one visit. For “which link you came from,” we record only tags we set (for example, one per video). We also measure usage with Google Analytics (see “Cookies and data sent to outside services” below).
  6. After paid service starts: payment details are collected by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not keep card numbers; we receive whether you paid, the amount, time, and country.

3. Why we use it

  1. To make and deliver your result (where you weren’t understood, why, a rephrased example, and a level estimate)
  2. To provide the Service, verify identity, and answer inquiries
  3. To prepare and improve the Service (including improving questions and scoring, training listeners, and our use of recordings, transcripts and results to build AI that assists scoring)
  4. To tell you when the Service is ready and send Service-related notices
  5. To analyze usage (as statistics that do not identify you)
  6. To prevent misuse
  7. After paid service starts, to charge and refund

We publish recordings or results as examples (for example, in videos) only with your separate consent.

4. Sharing with third parties

Except where required by law, we do not give your personal data to third parties without your consent.

5. Service providers and providers outside Japan

Test recordings, transcripts and results are stored on computers we manage in Japan. We also use the following providers, all based in the United States; your information is stored or processed on their servers.

For the U.S. privacy system, see the Personal Information Protection Commission’s materials (Japanese). We will tell you about these measures without delay upon request.

5-2. Cookies and data sent to outside services

To analyze site usage, we use Google Analytics by Google LLC. When you open a page, the following is sent to Google: the page URL, where you came from, time, browser and device type, screen size, IP address, and a cookie identifier for the visit. We do not send your name or email. Google handles this under its Privacy Policy.

For visits from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we do not use analytics cookies until you agree. To stop measurement, use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or turn off cookies in your browser. Your browser also stores your language setting and your consent choice.

6. How long we keep it

  • Sign-ups and inquiries: up to 5 years from collection
  • Recordings, transcripts and results: up to 5 years from collection, to provide and improve the Service
  • Site usage data: up to 2 years from collection

We delete information, including at our service providers and in backups, after these periods or once it is no longer needed. If you ask us to delete your data, we delete the original data and stop using it for training. However, removing one person’s influence from an AI model that has already been trained may be technically difficult.

7. How we protect it

  • We appoint a person responsible for personal information, set handling rules, and train staff.
  • Only staff who need access can access the data.
  • Devices that handle recordings are protected with passwords and encryption, and taking them out is controlled.
  • If a leak or similar incident occurs, we investigate promptly and report to the Personal Information Protection Commission and to you as required by law.
  • Listeners receive only the recordings they are assigned and agree to keep them confidential.
  • Connections to our database are encrypted, and browsers cannot read other people’s data.
  • We check the foreign systems and the providers’ measures described in section 5.

8. Your requests (access, correction, stopping use, deletion)

You may ask us to tell you our purposes, disclose, correct, add to, or delete your data, stop using it or sharing it, or disclose records of sharing. Email privacy@aunjapanese.com or use the contact form, from the email you registered. We will contact that email to confirm it is you. If you can no longer use that email, or if an agent is making the request, tell us through the form and we will confirm identity or authority another way. We try to disclose in the way you prefer (such as email), respond without delay, and tell you the reason if we cannot meet a request. There is no fee.
